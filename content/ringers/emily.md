Emily joined Bells of the Rockies in 2022. She has been ringing bells off and on since she was 9 years old in church
choirs across the state, and is excited to be part of such an excellent choir!

Emily and her husband live in Loveland with their daughter, their two dogs and their cat . She is currently enjoying
being a stay-at-home mom for her daughter. When she isn't being a mom, she enjoys knitting, crocheting, spinning yarn,
and all manner of fiber crafts.
