A self-proclaimed musical nerd with the skills to play over fifteen instruments, Brandi has loved bells since the first
time she played chimes her senior year of high school. Before ringing with the BotR, Brandi has rung with The
Bethany College Bell Choir (Lindsborg, KS), the Rocky Mountain Ringers, the Smithtonians and various church bell choirs
across the front range.

Brandi recently moved to Fort Collins to further her education and is thrilled to be ringing with the Bells of the
Rockies. Outside of bells she is active in church choirs, going to comic-cons and watching endless hours of Hallmark
movies.
