Chris Vickers has been a ringer with Bells of the Rockies since 1999. Her music minor from the University of Wisconsin -
River Falls includes tons of experience playing saxophone in quartet ensemble, marching band, concert band, and jazz
band. Bells of the Rockies, however, provides the ultimate in ensemble playing and continues to be a musical outlet and
means for challenge and growth.

Chris retired in 2023 after a 39-year career in math education. She lives in Greeley with her husband, Jeff.
