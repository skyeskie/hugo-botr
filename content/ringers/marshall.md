Marshall Jones began directing and ringing handbells in 2003. He has been ringing and directing in most of the years
since with the exception of time in grad school for degrees in composition.

He is currently music director at Plymouth Congregational UCC in Fort Collins. He has previously served churches in
Montana, Nebraska, Florida, North Carolina, and Colorado. He has also taught music theory and aural perception as well
as other music classes at Flathead Valley Community College and the University of Arkansas.
